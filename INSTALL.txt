Chordwright for Mac
===================

1. Unzip Chordwright-mac.zip so you have a folder called Chordwright-mac in Downloads.

2. Open Terminal (Cmd+Space, type Terminal) and paste these lines. It asks for your Mac password.

   cd ~/Downloads/Chordwright-mac
   sudo mkdir -p /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   sudo cp -R Chordwright.vst3 /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   sudo cp -R Chordwright.component /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
   sudo xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Chordwright.vst3 /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Chordwright.component

   The last line tells macOS the plugin is safe to load. Without it, macOS blocks
   plugins downloaded from the internet that are not signed by a paid developer account.

3. Restart Studio One. Chordwright shows up under Instruments, made by "Kristian".
   Use the VST3 version. Studio One only passes MIDI out of VST3 instruments.

Routing in Studio One
---------------------
1. Drag Chordwright onto the arrange view. That makes an instrument track. Your MIDI
   keyboard plays into this track.
2. Add your Arturia or Native Instruments sound on a second instrument track.
3. On that second track, set its input to Chordwright instead of your keyboard.
4. Turn on monitoring on both tracks. Arm the second track to record the chords as MIDI.

Play a key on your keyboard and you hear the chord through your synth.
